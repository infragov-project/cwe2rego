Below are **IaC‑relevant weaknesses/patterns** that indicate **CWE‑319 (Cleartext Transmission of Sensitive Information)**. These are the types of misconfigurations a scanner should flag, along with **keywords/attributes/fields** that typically imply the weakness (no specific IaC language mentioned).

---

## ✅ Weaknesses to Detect in IaC for CWE‑319

### 1) **Plaintext Protocols / Schemes Allowed**
**Evidence:** Endpoints or listeners use non‑TLS protocols for sensitive traffic.  
**Typical keywords/values:**
- `protocol: http`, `scheme: http`
- `ftp`, `telnet`, `smtp`, `pop3`, `imap`, `ldap`, `snmp`, `mqtt`
- `plaintext`, `cleartext`, `unencrypted`

**Common port indicators:**
- `80`, `21`, `23`, `25`, `110`, `143`, `389`, `1883`, `6379`, etc.

---

### 2) **Encryption in Transit Disabled or Not Enforced**
**Evidence:** A config flag explicitly turns off TLS/SSL or secure transfer.  
**Typical attributes/fields:**
- `https_only = false`
- `secure_transfer = disabled`
- `enforce_ssl = false`
- `require_tls = false`
- `tls_enabled = false`
- `ssl_enabled = false`
- `encryption_in_transit = false`
- `min_tls_version = none / 0`

---

### 3) **Insecure Listener / Endpoint Configuration**
**Evidence:** Service listener set to cleartext protocol or TLS not configured.  
**Keywords/fields:**
- `listener_protocol: HTTP`
- `load_balancer_protocol: http`
- `redirect_http_to_https = false`
- missing TLS certificate/key fields where HTTPS is expected

---

### 4) **Insecure Connection Strings or URLs**
**Evidence:** Service URL uses non‑TLS while carrying credentials.  
**Keywords/patterns:**
- `http://` with `password`, `secret`, `token`, `key`, `auth`
- `sslmode=disable`
- `use_ssl=false`
- `insecure_skip_verify=true`

---

### 5) **Management or Debug Interfaces over Cleartext**
**Evidence:** Administrative channels exposed without encryption.  
**Keywords/fields:**
- `management_protocol: telnet / http`
- `enable_remote_debug = true` (without TLS)
- admin ports mapped to plaintext services

---

## ✅ Summary Detection Rule (Short)
Flag configurations that **allow plaintext transport** or **disable TLS/SSL** for sensitive services, especially when credentials or sensitive data are involved.
