**CWE‑546: Suspicious Comment — IaC Detection Guidance**

**Weakness to detect**
- Presence of **comments or free‑text notes** that explicitly indicate *bugs, incomplete functionality, missing security controls, or temporary insecure workarounds*.  
- In IaC, this includes **inline comments**, **metadata notes**, **annotations**, **descriptions**, **labels**, or **tags** that carry developer remarks about unfinished or risky configuration.

---

### 🔎 Indicative Keywords / Phrases
Look for comment text or descriptive fields containing words such as:

**General “suspicious comment” markers**
- `TODO`, `FIXME`, `BUG`, `HACK`, `XXX`, `TEMP`, `LATER`, `WORKAROUND`

**Security‑related incomplete or risky notes**
- `remove later`, `temporary`, `for testing`, `not for prod`, `skip`, `ignore`, `bypass`, `disable`
- `open for now`, `allow all`, `public until`, `default password`, `hard‑coded`, `missing auth`
- `add encryption`, `add logging`, `add validation`, `tighten rules`, `restrict access`, `needs review`

**Explicit caution or insecurity**
- `insecure`, `unsafe`, `weak`, `dangerous`, `unprotected`, `no validation`, `no auth`

---

### 🧩 Fields / Attributes to Scan
Even outside explicit comments, IaC files often carry user‑provided text fields that can hide these clues:
- `description`
- `notes`
- `annotations`
- `metadata`
- `labels`
- `tags`
- `remarks`, `comment`, `doc`, or similar free‑text fields

---

✅ **Detection Requirement Summary:**  
Flag any IaC script where **comment text or descriptive metadata contains suspicious markers** indicating unfinished security work, hard‑coded values, missing checks, or temporary insecure configurations.
