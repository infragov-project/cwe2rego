Below are the **core weakness patterns** that indicate **CWE‑326: Inadequate Encryption Strength** when reviewing Infrastructure‑as‑Code (IaC) configurations. These patterns focus on **encryption settings that are theoretically sound but too weak for modern protection needs**.

---

## ✅ Weakness Patterns to Detect in IaC

### 1. **Weak or Deprecated Encryption Algorithms**
**What to look for:** encryption settings that explicitly select outdated or weak ciphers.

**Indicative keywords/fields**
- `encryption_algorithm`
- `cipher`
- `server_side_encryption`
- `kms_key_spec`
- `algorithm`

**Suspicious values**
- `DES`, `3DES`, `RC4`, `RC2`, `Blowfish`, `IDEA`, `XOR`
- `ECB` mode
- `MD5`, `SHA1` used for crypto protection

---

### 2. **Insufficient Key Length**
**What to look for:** key sizes below modern minimum recommendations.

**Indicative fields**
- `key_size`, `key_length`, `key_bits`
- `rsa_bits`, `aes_bits`, `key_spec`

**Weak values**
- Symmetric key sizes < 128/256
- RSA or DH sizes < 2048
- Elliptic curve sizes < 256

---

### 3. **Legacy TLS/SSL Protocols or Weak Security Policies**
**What to look for:** minimum protocol versions or policies that allow obsolete protocols.

**Indicative fields**
- `min_protocol_version`
- `tls_version`, `ssl_protocols`
- `security_policy`, `ssl_policy`
- `protocols`

**Weak values**
- `SSLv2`, `SSLv3`, `TLS1.0`, `TLS1.1`
- `legacy`, `compatibility`, `low`

---

### 4. **Weak Cipher Suites or Hash/MAC Algorithms Allowed**
**What to look for:** lists of allowed ciphers that include weak options.

**Indicative fields**
- `cipher_suites`, `allowed_ciphers`
- `ssl_ciphers`, `mac_algorithm`
- `hash_algorithm`

**Weak values**
- `RC4`, `DES`, `3DES`
- `MD5`, `SHA1`
- `NULL`, `EXPORT`, `aNULL`, `LOW`

---

### 5. **Static or Fixed IV/Salt/Nonce**
**What to look for:** non‑random cryptographic parameters that reduce effective strength.

**Indicative fields**
- `iv`, `initialization_vector`
- `salt`, `nonce`

**Weak indicators**
- hardcoded/constant values
- reuse of IV/salt across resources

---

## ✅ Summary
Any IaC configuration that **enables encryption but chooses weak algorithms, short keys, outdated TLS versions, or weak cipher suites** can indicate **CWE‑326**. These settings may still be “encrypted,” yet are **not strong enough for current security requirements**.
