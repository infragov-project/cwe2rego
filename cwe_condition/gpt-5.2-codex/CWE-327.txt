Below are **IaC‑centric indicators** that suggest **CWE‑327 (Use of a Broken or Risky Cryptographic Algorithm)**.  
These are generic patterns and **do not depend on any specific IaC language**.

---

## ✅ What to look for (weakness patterns)

### 1) **Algorithm selection uses deprecated/weak crypto**
**Fields/keywords likely involved:**  
`algorithm`, `encryption`, `cipher`, `crypto`, `hash`, `digest`, `signature`

**Risky values / indicators:**  
- Symmetric: `DES`, `3DES`, `RC4`, `RC2`, `Blowfish`, `IDEA`, `TEA`  
- Hashing: `MD2`, `MD4`, `MD5`, `SHA1`  
- Modes: `ECB`, `CBC` without integrity  
- “Obfuscation” instead of encryption: `xor`, `rot`, `base64`, `custom`

---

### 2) **TLS/SSL protocol versions allow legacy protocols**
**Fields/keywords likely involved:**  
`tls_version`, `ssl_version`, `min_protocol`, `enabled_protocols`, `security_policy`

**Risky values:**  
`SSLv2`, `SSLv3`, `TLS1.0`, `TLS1.1`, `legacy`, `compatibility`

---

### 3) **Cipher suite configuration includes weak ciphers**
**Fields/keywords likely involved:**  
`cipher_suites`, `allowed_ciphers`, `ciphers`

**Risky markers:**  
`NULL`, `EXPORT`, `ANON`, `MD5`, `RC4`, `DES`, `3DES`, `SHA1`, `CBC`

---

### 4) **Key size or key spec too small**
**Fields/keywords likely involved:**  
`key_size`, `key_length`, `key_bits`, `key_spec`, `rsa_bits`, `dsa_bits`, `curve`

**Risky patterns:**  
- RSA/DSA < **2048 bits**  
- ECC < **256 bits**  
- Symmetric keys < **128 bits**

---

### 5) **Password or integrity hashing uses weak algorithms**
**Fields/keywords likely involved:**  
`password_hash`, `hmac_algorithm`, `digest_algorithm`, `checksum`

**Risky values:**  
`MD5`, `SHA1`, `CRC`, `crypt` (legacy variants)

---

### 6) **Explicit “allow weak/legacy crypto” flags**
**Fields/keywords likely involved:**  
`allow_weak_crypto`, `legacy_crypto`, `compatibility_mode`, `enable_insecure`

---

## Summary
If IaC scripts **configure crypto or TLS settings** and those settings **select obsolete algorithms, weak hashes, legacy protocols, small key sizes, or insecure cipher suites**, they are strong indicators of **CWE‑327**.
