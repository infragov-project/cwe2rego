Below are **IaC weakness patterns** that imply **CWE‑284 (Improper Access Control)** and should be detected.  
Each item includes **generic keywords/attributes/fields** that commonly signal the issue (no language‑specific terms).

---

### 1) **Missing Authentication / Anonymous Access**
**Risk:** Resource is accessible without proving identity.  
**Indicative fields / keywords:**
- `public`, `anonymous`, `unauthenticated`, `guest`
- `auth = false`, `require_auth = false`, `authentication_disabled`
- `allow_anonymous`, `no_auth`, `open_access`

---

### 2) **Missing or Incorrect Authorization**
**Risk:** Identity is not checked for permission to access resource.  
**Indicative fields / keywords:**
- `effect: allow` without constraints
- `allow_all`, `default_allow`
- empty or missing `principal`, `member`, `role`, `condition`
- `resource: *`, `scope: global`

---

### 3) **Overly Permissive ACL/Policy**
**Risk:** Policies grant broad or unintended access.  
**Indicative fields / keywords:**
- `principal: *`, `allUsers`, `everyone`, `any`
- `action: *`, `full_access`, `admin`, `owner`, `superuser`
- `readWrite`, `write` for public or global principals

---

### 4) **World‑Readable or World‑Writable Permissions**
**Risk:** Critical data or resources exposed to all users.  
**Indicative fields / keywords:**
- `public_read`, `public_write`, `public_read_write`
- `full_control`, `rwx` for all, `mode: 0777`, `perm: 777`
- `everyone: read/write`

---

### 5) **Unrestricted Network Access / Open Exposure**
**Risk:** Resource reachable by any network actor.  
**Indicative fields / keywords:**
- `0.0.0.0/0`, `::/0`, `any`, `all`, `*`
- `allow_all`, `open_port`, `ingress_all`, `public_endpoint`
- `internet_facing`, `exposed`

---

### 6) **Over‑Trusted Role/Identity Assumption**
**Risk:** Arbitrary principals can assume privileged roles.  
**Indicative fields / keywords:**
- `trust: *`, `any_principal`, `external`, `federated`
- `assume_role` without restrictions
- `root` or `admin` allowed broadly

---

### 7) **No Accountability / Audit Disabled**
**Risk:** Actions not tracked, enabling abuse.  
**Indicative fields / keywords:**
- `audit: false`, `logging: disabled`, `monitoring: off`
- `no_audit`, `disable_logging`

---

### How this maps to CWE‑284
These patterns commonly reflect:
- **Missing Authorization / Incorrect Authorization**
- **Incorrect Permission Assignment**
- **Missing Authentication**
- **Improper Restriction of Communication Channels**

Detecting the above **misconfigurations in IaC** strongly indicates **Improper Access Control (CWE‑284)**.
