Below are **IaC‑relevant weakness indicators** that commonly signal **CWE‑250: Execution with Unnecessary Privileges**. These are generic patterns and terms (not tied to any specific language) that you can scan for in infrastructure definitions.

---

## ✅ Weaknesses to Detect for CWE‑250 in IaC

### 1. **Workloads Running as Over‑Privileged Accounts**
**Indicators**
- Execution user set to **root / administrator / system**
- Explicit **UID/GID = 0**
- `runAs` or `user` fields pointing to admin accounts  
**Keywords/Fields**
- `root`, `administrator`, `system`, `superuser`
- `runAsUser`, `user`, `uid`, `gid`, `serviceUser`

---

### 2. **Privileged or Escalated Execution Flags**
**Indicators**
- Privilege escalation allowed
- Full OS capabilities enabled  
**Keywords/Fields**
- `privileged`, `allowPrivilegeEscalation`
- `capabilities: ALL`, `add: ["ALL"]`
- `setuid`, `setgid`, `sudo`, `NOPASSWD`

---

### 3. **Over‑Broad IAM / Role Permissions**
**Indicators**
- Roles or policies grant excessive privileges to workloads  
**Keywords/Fields**
- `Administrator`, `Owner`, `FullAccess`, `SuperUser`
- `role`, `policy`, `permissions`, `scope`, `binding`
- `cluster-admin`, `system:admin`, `allAccess`

---

### 4. **Wildcard or Global Permission Grants**
**Indicators**
- Permissions allow “everything” or apply to all resources  
**Keywords/Fields**
- `*`, `all`, `any`, `global`, `allResources`
- `actions: "*"`, `resources: "*"`  
- `effect: Allow` with no restriction/conditions

---

### 5. **High‑Privilege Role Binding to Service Accounts or Instances**
**Indicators**
- Service accounts or compute workloads tied to admin roles  
**Keywords/Fields**
- `serviceAccount`, `principal`, `member`, `instanceProfile`
- `roleBinding`, `assumeRole`, `passRole`

---

### 6. **Host‑Level Access for Containers/Workloads**
**Indicators**
- Access to host namespaces or filesystem (elevated control)  
**Keywords/Fields**
- `hostNetwork`, `hostPID`, `hostIPC`, `hostPath`
- `mount: /`, `mount: /var/run`, `device`, `sysAdmin`

---

### 7. **Application Uses Superuser DB / Storage Accounts**
**Indicators**
- App or workload configured with DB admin credentials  
**Keywords/Fields**
- `adminUser`, `rootUser`, `superuser`
- `grant all privileges`, `full control`

---

### 8. **Default or Built‑In Admin Accounts Enabled**
**Indicators**
- Built‑in admin login enabled for general workload use  
**Keywords/Fields**
- `enableRootLogin`, `allowAdminLogin`
- `defaultAdmin`, `bootstrapAdmin`

---

## Summary (What to Flag)
Look for **execution identities or permissions that exceed operational need**, especially:
- **Admin/superuser accounts**
- **Wildcard or global permissions**
- **Privilege escalation flags**
- **Unrestricted role bindings**

These patterns strongly suggest **Execution with Unnecessary Privileges (CWE‑250)**.
