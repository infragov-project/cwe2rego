Below are the **key weakness indicators** to detect CWE‑1327 (“Binding to an Unrestricted IP Address”) in Infrastructure‑as‑Code (IaC) scripts. The goal is to find configurations where a service or resource is bound to **all interfaces** instead of a specific network interface.

---

## ✅ Necessary Weakness to Detect
**A network‑exposed service or resource is configured to bind/listen on all interfaces (unrestricted address).**

---

## 🔎 Indicators / Keywords / Attributes to Flag  
*(language‑agnostic; focus on common IaC patterns)*

### 1) **Explicit wildcard address values**
Look for any of these values in fields that define a bind or listen address:

- `0.0.0.0`
- `0.0.0.0/0`
- `::` or `::/0` (IPv6 wildcard)
- `*`
- `any`
- `all`

### 2) **Binding/listening fields**
These fields frequently define where a service listens or binds:

- `listen`, `listen_address`
- `bind`, `bind_address`
- `host`, `server_address`
- `interface`
- `ip`, `address`
- `client_address`, `advertise_address`
- `endpoint_address`
- `mgmt_address`, `admin_address`

### 3) **Public exposure flags**
Fields suggesting exposure on all interfaces or internet‑facing:

- `public`, `publicly_accessible`
- `expose`, `exposed`
- `open`, `open_to_public`
- `allow_all`, `allow_any`
- `listen_on_all_interfaces`

### 4) **Implicit “all interfaces”**
Even without an explicit IP:
- **Empty/omitted bind/listen field** when defaults are known to bind to all interfaces.

### 5) **Ingress/source CIDR + bind all**
If a service both:
- binds to wildcard address **and**
- uses source ranges such as `0.0.0.0/0` or `::/0`,
it strengthens the finding.

---

## ✅ Summary Detection Rule (Conceptual)
> **Flag any IaC resource that configures a service/listener to bind or listen on `0.0.0.0`, `::`, or any “all/any/*” address, especially for databases, APIs, dashboards, or management interfaces.**
